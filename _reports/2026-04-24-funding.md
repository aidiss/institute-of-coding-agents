# Funding: AI Coding Agent Space
*Report date: 2026-04-24*

## Overview

The final week of April 2026 brought two headline-grabbing valuation resets: Cursor is in advanced talks for a $2B raise at a $50B+ pre-money valuation — nearly doubling its November 2025 post-money mark of $29.3B — and Cognition AI is in early talks to raise at a $25B valuation, more than doubling its September 2025 high of $10.2B. Factory confirmed a $150M close at a $1.5B enterprise-focused unicorn valuation. Macro backdrop: global VC hit $300B in Q1 2026 alone, with AI accounting for 80% ($242B) of that total.

## Acquisitions & Exits

### Meta — Dreamer AI Acqui-Hire (March 2026)
Meta acquired the full founding team and key personnel of **Dreamer**, a high-profile agentic AI startup, in an acqui-hire. All three co-founders — **David Singleton**, **Hugo Barra**, and **Nicholas Jitkoff** — joined Meta's **Superintelligence Labs** division. Terms undisclosed. The move is part of Meta's push to close the gap between LLMs and fully autonomous AI systems. *Confirmed.*

### OpenAI — Six Acquisitions in Early 2026
OpenAI has closed six acquisitions in the first quarter of 2026, nearly matching its full-year 2025 total. Among them: an **acqui-hire of OpenClaw**, an open-source AI agent project built by Austrian developer **Peter Steinberger**. Broader consolidation continues across the lab tier, with FTC scrutiny of acqui-hire structures intensifying. *Confirmed (aggregate count); individual deal terms largely undisclosed.*

### Nvidia — Groq Deal (~$20B, reported)
Nvidia's reported ~$20B deal with Groq has been characterized by some sources as an acqui-hire structured to bring inference infrastructure talent in-house. Deal terms and structure remain unconfirmed. *Rumored/unconfirmed.*
