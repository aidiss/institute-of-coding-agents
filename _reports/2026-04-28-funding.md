## Acquisitions & Exits

### SpaceX option to acquire Cursor for $60B (Confirmed — Apr 22–23, 2026)

SpaceX struck a deal giving it the right to acquire Anysphere (Cursor) for **$60 billion** at any point before end of 2026 (likely timed around the SpaceX Nasdaq IPO targeting June 2026 at a $1.75T valuation). If SpaceX does not exercise the option, it pays Anysphere **$10 billion** for joint development work. The arrangement preempted Cursor's in-progress $2B fundraise at a $50B valuation.

Key detail: SpaceX announced the deal on X ahead of a New York Times story that had framed it as a completed acquisition. CEO Michael Truell (25, MIT dropout) confirmed via X that the partnership focuses on scaling Composer, Anysphere's own model. Strategic rationale for Cursor: compute bottleneck. Strategic rationale for SpaceX/xAI (merged Feb 2026 at ~$1.25T combined valuation): xAI had no competitive AI coding product.

### Windsurf (Codeium) — Three-way split (2025, background context)

1. **OpenAI** originally agreed to acquire Windsurf for ~$3B (Apr 2025). Deal collapsed when Microsoft blocked full IP transfer.
2. **Google** secured a $2.4B licensing + talent deal (Jul 2025): ~$1.2B to investors, ~$1.2B in compensation for ~40 hires including co-founders Varun Mohan and Douglas Chen to Google DeepMind.
3. **Cognition** acquired the remaining Windsurf product, brand, IP, and remaining team. Cognition ARR more than doubled post-acquisition.

### Meta acqui-hires Dreamer AI (Mar 23, 2026)

Meta hired the entire team of **Dreamer**, an agentic AI startup building cross-device personal agent infrastructure, into **Meta Superintelligence Labs** (led by Chief AI Officer Alexandr Wang, former Scale AI founder). Dreamer's technology is licensed to Meta beyond a pure talent grab. Undisclosed price.

### OpenAI acqui-hires OpenClaw / Peter Steinberger (Feb 15, 2026)

Sam Altman recruited **Peter Steinberger** (creator of OpenClaw, a viral open-source AI personal agent) to lead next-generation personal agents at OpenAI. Meta and Microsoft also competed for the hire. OpenClaw remains open-source under MIT licence under an OpenAI-sponsored foundation.

## Regulatory Notes

- **FTC** (Chair Andrew Ferguson) opened an investigation in Jan 2026 into acqui-hires industry-wide, characterising them as "mergers in disguise" that evade antitrust review. The Microsoft–Inflection AI deal is under active investigation.
- The SpaceX–Cursor structure (pay $10B or buy for $60B) is being watched as a novel contingent acquisition form; congressional scrutiny of acqui-hires remains ongoing.
