### Notes

**CopilotKit** — Seattle-based; offers AG-UI / "agent protocol" infrastructure that lets product teams embed coding-style AI agents inside their apps. Round combines a fresh $20M Series A with a previously unannounced $7M seed. Stated enterprise customers include Deutsche Telekom, Docusign, Cisco, S&P Global. Funds will go to headcount expansion (currently ~25 employees) and the new self-hostable "Enterprise Intelligence" SKU. Valuation not disclosed.

**Nova Intelligence** — UK-based; agentic AI platform aimed at the $89B SAP S/4HANA migration market (analyzes legacy ABAP code, generates configuration, mapping, and tests). Total funding now >$40M. Co-founder lineup combines DeepMind/Meta AI research (Emma Qian, CEO), SAP HANA co-inventor Alexander Zeier, and former Cargill CIO Justin Kershaw — a deliberate pairing of agent research with enterprise SAP credibility.

**Sierra** (carried from May 5 brief) — Confirmed $950M at $15.8B post-money. Bret Taylor / Clay Bavor company, focused on customer-service agents but ships a developer-facing **Agent SDK** that overlaps the coding-agent stack. $150M ARR cited at announcement, customers said to include nearly half the Fortune 50.

**Parallel Web Systems** — Adjacent to coding agents (web-grounding infrastructure for agent runtimes), but increasingly load-bearing for the category. Parag Agrawal's (ex-Twitter CEO) startup tripled its valuation in five months on the back of >100K developer signups and design-partner deals with Harvey, Attio, Modal, Rogo.

**Cursor / Anysphere** — Status unchanged from May 5: the standalone $2B / $50B+ raise remains formally pending and has not been announced as closed. The SpaceX option deal (Apr 21) — $60B acquisition right or $10B JV-fee alternative by year-end — pre-empts a clean close; LPs and bankers are reportedly waiting for the SpaceX optionality to resolve.

**Cognition** — No movement since the Apr 23 Bloomberg report. Round still characterized as "early talks" at $25B target (vs. $10.2B in Sept 2025 Founders Fund-led round). Devin ARR last cited at $73M (June 2025).
