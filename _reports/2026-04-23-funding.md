### Deal Notes

**Cursor / Anysphere** — ARR reached $2B by February 2026, making it the fastest B2B software company in history to hit that milestone from zero (~3 years). The company forecasts $6B+ ARR by end of 2026. The new $2B round at a $50B+ valuation would represent a 70%+ step-up from the November 2025 Series D at $29.3B. Round not yet closed as of April 23.

**Replit** — Valuation tripled in six months (from $3B to $9B). Platform reports 50M+ users and Fortune 500 penetration at 85%. Targeting $1B ARR by end of 2026. Alongside the funding, launched Agent 4 (claimed 10x faster than Agent 3).

**Factory** — Founded 2023 by Matan Grinberg and Eno Reyes. Product ("Droids") covers testing, review, documentation, and deployment in addition to code generation. Model-agnostic architecture switches between Claude, DeepSeek, and others. Revenue doubled month-over-month for six consecutive months. Enterprise customers include Nvidia, Adobe, EY, Palo Alto Networks, Adyen, MongoDB, Bayer, and Zapier.

**Entire** — New company from former GitHub CEO Thomas Dohmke (see Talent Moves). First product is Checkpoints, which captures prompts, reasoning, and decisions behind AI-generated code.

## Acquisitions & Exits

**Meta / Dreamer** (Confirmed, March 23 2026)
Meta acqui-hired the entire founding team and key personnel of Dreamer, a startup focused on agentic AI. Terms not disclosed.

**Meta / Manus** (Confirmed, announced Dec 2025)
Meta agreed to acquire Manus for $2B. Manus built autonomous agents that operate a web browser to complete tasks (hotel bookings, reservations, etc.).

**OpenAI / OpenClaw** (Confirmed, February 2026)
OpenAI acqui-hired OpenClaw, an open-source AI agent project built by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger. One of six acquisitions OpenAI closed in early 2026, nearly matching its full-year 2025 total.

**Nvidia / Groq** (Confirmed, announced Dec 24 2025)
Structured as a $20B non-exclusive licensing deal for Groq's inference chip architecture rather than a traditional acquisition. Nvidia also hired the majority of Groq's engineering team (see Talent Moves).
