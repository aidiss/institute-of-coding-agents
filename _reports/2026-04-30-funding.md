# Funding: AI Coding Agent Space
*Report date: 2026-04-30*

## Overview

The final days of April 2026 close out a month dominated by two narratives: Cursor's escalating valuation saga (from a $2B raise at $50B to a SpaceX acquisition option at $60B), and the geopolitical dimension entering AI M&A (China blocking Meta's purchase of Manus). Meanwhile, enterprise-focused pure-plays—Factory, Cognition—are closing capital and expanding geography, signaling that the market has moved past the IDE-assistant phase into autonomous agent infrastructure.

### Key Revenue & Usage Milestones

- **Anysphere (Cursor):** $2B ARR as of February 2026 (zero to $2B in ~3 years — fastest B2B scaling on record); management forecast $6B+ ARR by year-end. 1M+ paying customers; 70% of Fortune 1,000 in customer base. Previous valuation: $29.3B post-money (Series D, Nov 2025).
- **Factory:** Revenue doubling every month for six consecutive months as of April 2026. Enterprise customers include Nvidia, Adobe, EY, Palo Alto Networks, Adyen, MongoDB, Bayer, Zapier, Morgan Stanley.
- **Cognition (Devin):** Grew from $1M to $73M ARR in nine months (Sep 2024 → Jun 2025). Post-Windsurf acquisition added ~$82M ARR (enterprise revenue doubling quarterly).
- **Claude Code (Anthropic):** Reached $1B annualized revenue run rate within its first six months of public availability.
- **Emergent:** Claims $50M ARR and 5M+ users across 190+ countries as of January 2026; targeting $100M ARR by April 2026.

## Acquisitions & Exits

### SpaceX — Cursor Acquisition Option (Confirmed, April 21, 2026)

SpaceX announced a deal granting it the right to **acquire Anysphere (Cursor) for $60B** at a future date this year, or alternatively pay **$10B for joint development work**. The $10B payment is guaranteed regardless of whether SpaceX exercises the acquisition option. The deal effectively halted Cursor's nearly closed $2B financing round (led by a16z and Thrive Capital), which had been on track to close the week of April 21.

Strategic context: xAI merged with SpaceX in February 2026 (combined valuation ~$1.25T). The deal pairs Cursor's IDE and Composer model with xAI's Colossus GPU cluster in Memphis (equivalent capacity described as 1M H100s). Elon Musk framed the deal as xAI needing a leading coding tool after falling behind competitors. *(Confirmed — Bloomberg, TechCrunch, CNBC)*

### China Blocks Meta — Manus Acquisition (April 27, 2026)

Chinese regulators ordered Meta to abandon its acquisition of **Manus**, the AI agent developer, approximately five months after the deal was announced. The acquisition had been valued at **$2–3B**. Manus, which went viral in early 2025 for its autonomous agent demos, was developed by a Chinese team and maintained significant operations in China, triggering regulatory review. The blocking mirrors China's 2021 intervention in Nvidia's Arm acquisition attempt. *(Confirmed — SiliconAngle, April 27, 2026)*

### Anthropic — Vercept Acquisition (February 25, 2026)

Anthropic acquired **Vercept**, a Seattle-based AI startup that built "computer use" agents capable of operating desktop applications. Vercept had raised $50M total prior to acquisition. The deal advances Claude's ability to complete multi-step tasks inside live applications (navigating spreadsheets, managing workflows). Co-founders **Kiana Ehsani**, **Luca Weihs**, and **Ross Girshick** joined Anthropic; Vercept's consumer app **Vy** shut down within 30 days of the deal. *(Confirmed — Anthropic, TechCrunch, GeekWire, Feb 25, 2026)*

*Note: Co-founder **Matt Deitke** had already departed mid-2025 to Meta's Superintelligence Lab, reportedly offered $250M over four years.*

### Cognition — Havana Acquisition (Confirmed, April 2026)

As part of its Asia-Pacific expansion, Cognition acquired **Havana**, a Singapore-based AI agent startup. Havana's team is being integrated into Cognition's new APAC hub in Singapore's Central Business District, staffing engineering, product, sales, and marketing functions. *(Confirmed — TNGlobal, April 29, 2026)*

### Broader Trends

- Enterprise software executives (Salesforce, Snowflake, Datadog) are the primary target pool for OpenAI and Anthropic, valued for existing enterprise relationships over pure technical background.
- The Deitke/Vercept pattern — a founder poached by a hyperscaler at a price that triggers the startup's full acqui-hire — is being watched as a potential playbook for talent extraction.
- AI coding tools market overall estimated at **$12.8B in 2026** (up from $5.1B in 2024); 84% of developers now using or planning to use AI coding tools.

## Notes

- **Cursor / SpaceX deal:** The $60B figure is an option price (not a closed acquisition). SpaceX may instead pay $10B and walk away. No definitive close date announced.
- **Meta / Manus:** Deal collapse leaves Manus without a strategic acquirer; unclear whether Manus will seek alternative buyers or remain independent.
- **Anthropic / Vercept:** Terms undisclosed. Categorized as a capability-driven acqui-hire rather than a product acquisition (Vy was shut down).
- **Factory Series C:** Previous valuation not publicly disclosed; $1.5B appears to be the first public valuation anchor for the company.
- **Emergent:** $300M valuation is up from ~$100M seven months prior (tripled); ARR claims are self-reported and unaudited.
