### Cursor — $2B round at $50B+ valuation *(unconfirmed, deal terms still in flux)*

As of April 19, Anysphere (Cursor) is in active talks to close a **$2 billion round at a pre-money valuation exceeding $50 billion**, according to CNBC and Bloomberg. The proposed valuation nearly doubles the $29.3B post-money valuation set in November 2025. The round is reportedly oversubscribed, though terms remain unfinalized.

**Investors:** Returning leads Thrive Capital and Andreessen Horowitz; Nvidia (strategic); Battery Ventures (new entrant). Prior investors include Accel, DST Global, Coatue, and Google.

**Revenue trajectory (ARR):**
- Jan 2025 — $100M
- Jun 2025 — $500M
- Nov 2025 — $1B
- Feb 2026 — $2B
- End-2026 — $6B+ (internal projection)

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/19/cursor-ai-2-billion-funding-round.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/sources-cursor-in-talks-to-raise-2b-at-50b-valuation-as-enterprise-growth-surges/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-17/ai-coding-startup-cursor-in-talks-to-raise-2-billion-in-funding)*

### Replit — $400M Series D at $9B valuation *(confirmed, March 2026)*

Replit closed a **$400 million Series D** at a **$9 billion valuation**, a 3× step-up from its $3 billion valuation just six months earlier. The round was led by Georgian Partners with participation from a broad syndicate including Coatue, a16z, Craft Ventures, YC, Accenture Ventures, Okta Ventures, Databricks Ventures, G Squared, and Prysm Capital. Angel participants include Shaquille O'Neal and Jared Leto.

- **Product:** Replit Agent 4 launched alongside the round, combining design and code in a single environment
- **Revenue target:** On track for $1B run-rate revenue by end of 2026
- **Previous valuation:** $3B (Sep 2025)

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/11/replit-snags-9b-valuation-6-months-after-hitting-3b/), [Replit blog](https://blog.replit.com/replit-raises-400-million-dollars)*

### Poolside — up to $1B strategic investment from Nvidia at $12B valuation *(confirmed, Oct 2025)*

Nvidia committed up to **$1 billion** in Poolside, valuing the enterprise coding model startup at **$12 billion** — roughly 4× its previous valuation. Poolside builds specialized LLMs trained exclusively on software engineering tasks and deploys them within customer environments.

- **Total raised to date:** ~$1.6B (including a $500M Series B from Bain Capital in Oct 2024)
- **Previous valuation:** ~$3B at Series B
- **Model approach:** Foundation models trained "from the start" as software development assistants, using natural-language-to-code reinforcement learning

*Sources: [TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/nvidia-prepares-up-to-1b-investment-as-poolsides-valuation-jumps-to-12b/), [mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/nvidia-reportedly-investing-up-to-1-billion-in-ai-startup-poolside/)*

## Acquisitions & Exits

### Meta acqui-hires Dreamer *(confirmed, March 2026)*

Meta acquired the full founding team and key personnel of **Dreamer**, a high-profile agentic AI startup, in a March 2026 acqui-hire. The Dreamer team — including former Meta and Google executive **Hugo Barra** — will work within Meta's **Superintelligence Labs** group under Chief AI Officer **Alexandr Wang** (founder of Scale AI).

- **Target:** Dreamer (agentic AI startup)
- **Buyer:** Meta Superintelligence Labs
- **Terms:** Not disclosed; structured as acqui-hire of founding team and key personnel
- **Notable hire:** Hugo Barra (Dreamer), former VP at Meta and VP of Global at Xiaomi

*Sources: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/23/meta-acqui-hires-co-founders-agentic-ai-startup-dreamer/), [creati.ai](https://creati.ai/ai-news/2026-03-24/meta-acqui-hires-dreamer-ai-startup-agentic-ai-agents/)*

---

### Nvidia / Groq — acqui-hire dressed as a licensing deal *(confirmed, 2026)*

Nvidia hired **Jonathan Ross** (Groq founder and CEO), **Sunny Madra** (Groq president), and the majority of Groq's engineering talent. The deal is publicly framed as a licensing arrangement but is widely characterized as a de-facto acqui-hire. Groq's AI inference chips had been a competitive threat to Nvidia's data center business.

*Source: [Techi.com](https://www.techi.com/nvidia-groq-deal-acqui-hire-ai-inference/)*

---

### Prior context: Windsurf / Google / Cognition *(July 2025)*

OpenAI originally agreed to buy Windsurf (formerly Codeium) for **$3 billion** in May 2025. The deal collapsed when Microsoft objected to IP rights provisions. Google then signed a **$2.4 billion deal** to hire Windsurf's leadership and license its technology. Cognition subsequently acquired Windsurf's remaining entity (IP, product, remaining staff) for approximately **$250 million**.

### Hugo Barra → Meta Superintelligence Labs

Hugo Barra, a veteran of Meta's VR division and former VP Global at Xiaomi (and earlier VP of Android at Google), joined Meta's AI research arm as part of the Dreamer acqui-hire. His arrival is notable as Meta's Superintelligence Labs, led by Alexandr Wang, builds toward autonomous agent systems. Barra brings deep experience operating at the intersection of consumer hardware and AI.

### Jonathan Ross & Sunny Madra → Nvidia

Groq's founder and its president moved to Nvidia, bringing most of the engineering team. Groq had raised over $1B and was a leading alternative for high-throughput LLM inference; the move effectively neutralizes a competitor and brings inference optimization expertise in-house to Nvidia.
