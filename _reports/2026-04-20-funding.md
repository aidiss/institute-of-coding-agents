### Cursor — context
Cursor reached **$2B ARR** in February 2026, up from $1B ARR in November 2025 and $500M in June 2025. Previous valuation: $29.3B (Series D, November 2025, $2.3B raise). The proposed Series E would nearly double that. The company projects >$6B ARR by end of 2026. Lead investors Thrive Capital and a16z are returning backers. Nvidia is a new strategic participant. Gross margin recently turned slightly positive after introduction of a proprietary Composer model and use of lower-cost models (e.g., Kimi).

### Replit — context
Previous valuation: $3B (mid-2025). The Series D tripled valuation in six months. Replit has 150,000+ paying customers, generated $240M in revenue in 2025, and is targeting $1B ARR by end of 2026. Notable: round included non-traditional investors (Shaquille O'Neal, Jared Leto).

### Factory — context
Founded 2023 by Matan Grinberg and Eno Reyes. Its "Droids" platform goes beyond code generation to cover testing, code review, documentation, and deployment. Customers include Nvidia, Adobe, EY, Palo Alto Networks, and Adyen. Revenue has doubled month-over-month for six consecutive months. Model-agnostic (supports Anthropic, DeepSeek, others). Keith Rabois (Khosla MD) joined the board as part of this round.

## Acquisitions & Exits

### Cognition acquires Windsurf (July 2025 — confirmed)
Cognition, maker of AI coding agent Devin, acquired what remained of Windsurf for an estimated **$250M**. The deal followed Google's **$2.4B reverse-acquihire** of Windsurf's CEO Varun Mohan, co-founder Douglas Chen, and research leadership. The Windsurf acquisition more than doubled Cognition's ARR. Cognition subsequently raised **$400M at a $10.2B valuation** (September 2025) to fund growth post-acquisition.

### Meta acqui-hires Dreamer team (March 23, 2026 — confirmed)
Meta hired the entire founding team of agentic AI startup **Dreamer** into its **Superintelligence Labs** division. The three co-founders joining Meta are:
- **David Singleton** (former Google)
- **Hugo Barra** (former Xiaomi, Facebook VR)
- **Nicholas Jitkoff** (former Google, Stripe)

This was Meta's third major AI talent acquisition in four months.

### OpenAI acqui-hires OpenClaw / Peter Steinberger (February 14, 2026 — confirmed)
**Peter Steinberger**, Austrian developer and creator of open-source personal agent **OpenClaw**, joined OpenAI's **Codex team** to "drive the next generation of personal agents." Sam Altman personally announced the hire on X, calling Steinberger "a genius with a lot of amazing ideas about the future of very smart agents interacting with each other." OpenClaw itself moved to a foundation and remains open source, with OpenAI providing continued support.

*Note: OpenAI made six acquisitions in 2026 as of March, nearly matching its eight for all of 2025. The FTC announced in January 2026 it would investigate acqui-hire practices across the tech industry.*
