# Funding: AI Coding Agent Space
*Report date: 2026-05-03*

## Overview

Late April 2026 brought a cluster of major moves: Factory closed a confirmed Series C, while Cursor and Cognition are both in active fundraising talks at valuations roughly double their last marks. Cursor crossed **$2B ARR** — the fastest B2B software company to reach that milestone — as the broader AI coding agent sector continues to attract capital at a pace unmatched in enterprise software history.

### Revenue Milestones

- **Cursor (Anysphere):** Surpassed **$2B ARR** by February 2026, doubling from $1B in approximately 60 days. Projects a **$6B run rate** by end of 2026 — a tripling within a calendar year. Fastest B2B company in history from zero to $2B ARR (~3 years).
- **Cognition (Devin):** Grew from $1M to **$73M ARR** in nine months — fastest revenue ramp in coding agent history. Combined ARR with Windsurf is estimated at ~$150–155M.
- **Claude Code (Anthropic):** Run-rate estimated at **$2.5B ARR** as of February 2026. Anthropic closed a $30B Series G at $380B post-money in February 2026.
- **Replit:** Closed a $400M round at $9B valuation (confirmed March 2026); targeting **$1B ARR** before end of 2026.
- **Poolside:** Total raised ~$2.6B across rounds since 2023, second-most capitalized player in the space behind Cursor.

## Acquisitions & Exits

### Meta Acqui-Hires Dreamer (March 23, 2026) — Confirmed

Meta Platforms acquired the co-founders and team of **Dreamer**, an agentic AI startup, for an undisclosed sum. The team — including former Meta and Google executive **Hugo Barra** — joined Meta's Superintelligence Labs group under Chief AI Officer **Alexandr Wang** (former Scale AI founder). The Dreamer team is Meta's fifth notable acqui-hire into its Superintelligence Labs group in 2026.

### OpenAI Acqui-Hires OpenClaw (February 2026) — Confirmed

OpenAI acquired **OpenClaw**, an open-source AI coding agent built by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger, whose viral tool had gained significant traction in the developer community. Terms undisclosed. OpenAI has now closed six acquisitions in early 2026, nearly matching its full 2025 total.

### Bret Taylor's Sierra Acquires Fragment (April 23, 2026) — Confirmed

**Sierra** (Bret Taylor's AI customer experience company) acquired **Fragment**, a YC-backed AI startup. Fragment is not a pure coding agent play, but the deal is notable given Taylor's deep developer-tools ties and Sierra's potential to expand into agentic coding workflows. Terms undisclosed.

*Note: The Windsurf three-way split (OpenAI collapse / Google $2.4B acquihire / Cognition acquisition) closed in July–December 2025 and was covered in prior reports; see 2026-04-01-funding.md for full detail.*

Thesis: as most code shifts to AI-generated, developers need tooling to capture context, prompts, and reasoning behind agent output.
