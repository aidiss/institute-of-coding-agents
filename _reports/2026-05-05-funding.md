**Sierra** is an enterprise-focused AI agent platform (customer service / operations, not purely coding), included here given cross-over with agentic infrastructure.

## Acquisitions & Exits

### Windsurf (Codeium) — Three-Way Carve-Up (2025–2026)
The most significant saga in the space:

1. **May 2025**: OpenAI agreed to acquire Windsurf for $3B — its largest-ever acquisition.
2. **Deal collapse**: OpenAI and Microsoft disagreed over IP rights. Microsoft expected Windsurf's technology to fall under its existing OpenAI partnership agreement; OpenAI refused, viewing it as a competitive asset against GitHub Copilot. Exclusivity expired.
3. **Google countermove**: Google hired Windsurf CEO **Varun Mohan** and co-founder **Douglas Chen**, along with select R&D staff, for its DeepMind division in a ~$2.4B talent deal.
4. **Cognition acquires the rest**: Cognition acquired Windsurf's remaining entity, IP, and product, bringing on all remaining staff. Estimated price: ~$250M.

### Meta acqui-hires Dreamer AI (March 2026)
Meta acquired the co-founders and team of **Dreamer**, an agentic AI startup, for an undisclosed sum. The team (including former Meta/Google executive **Hugo Barra**) joined Meta's Superintelligence Labs group. Dreamer had focused on autonomous agent development.

### OpenAI — Six Acquisitions in Q1 2026
OpenAI has completed six acquisitions in early 2026, nearly matching its full 2025 total. Notable: the acqui-hire of **OpenClaw**, an open-source AI agent built by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger. Regulatory scrutiny is rising — the FTC announced in January 2026 it would investigate acqui-hires industry-wide as potential "mergers in disguise," with the Microsoft-Inflection deal already under active investigation.

## Market Context

- Coding and developer-tool AI startups collectively raised **over $3B** in Q4 2025–Q1 2026, making software development the highest-funded AI sector.
- Average round size for agentic AI startups nearly doubled YoY: $155M average for rounds closed in Q4 2025 / early 2026, vs. $82M in H1 2025.
- Acqui-hires are no longer distress sales — the Windsurf, Dreamer, Inflection, and Adept deals represent a new category of strategic talent acquisition at billion-dollar price points.
