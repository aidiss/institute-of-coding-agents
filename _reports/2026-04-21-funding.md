### Cursor — $2B round at $50B+ valuation *(unconfirmed, in talks)*

Anysphere, maker of Cursor, is in talks to raise a **$2 billion round at a pre-money valuation exceeding $50 billion** — nearly double the $29.3B post-money valuation assigned in November 2025. Returning investors Thrive Capital and Andreessen Horowitz are expected to lead; Nvidia is expected to write a strategic check; Battery Ventures is a potential new entrant. Existing investors include Accel, DST Global, Coatue, and Google.

**Revenue trajectory** (ARR):
- Jan 2025 — $100M
- Jun 2025 — $500M
- Nov 2025 — $1B
- Feb 2026 — $2B

That zero-to-$2B ARR arc in roughly three years is cited as the fastest B2B software scaling on record, ahead of Slack, Zoom, and Snowflake. Internal projections target a $6B run rate by end of 2026.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/19/cursor-ai-2-billion-funding-round.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/sources-cursor-in-talks-to-raise-2b-at-50b-valuation-as-enterprise-growth-surges/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-17/ai-coding-startup-cursor-in-talks-to-raise-2-billion-in-funding)*

### Factory — $150M Series C at $1.5B valuation *(confirmed, April 16 2026)*

Factory, founded in 2023 by **Matan Grinberg and Eno Reyes**, raised a $150M Series C led by Khosla Ventures. The company builds "Droids" — AI agents that handle code generation, testing, review, documentation, and deployment end-to-end for enterprise engineering teams.

- **Customers:** Nvidia, Adobe, EY, Palo Alto Networks, Adyen, MongoDB, Bayer, Zapier
- **Growth:** Revenue doubled month-over-month for each of the prior six months
- **Previous valuation:** Not publicly disclosed pre-Series C
- **Board addition:** Keith Rabois (Khosla Ventures MD) joined the board
- **Use of funds:** R&D, product development, international expansion

*Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/factory-hits-1-5b-valuation-to-build-ai-coding-for-enterprises/), [TechFundingNews](https://techfundingnews.com/factory-ai-150m-series-c-unicorn-ai-agents-developers/)*

## Acquisitions & Exits

### OpenAI acquires Astral *(announced March 19, 2026 — pending close)*

OpenAI announced the acquisition of **Astral**, creators of the widely used open-source Python developer tools **uv**, **Ruff**, and **ty**. Astral's tooling, written in Rust, delivers 10–100× faster performance than typical Python tools and serves several million developers.

- **Strategic rationale:** Astral's team joins the **Codex** team to extend the coding agent's reach across the full development lifecycle — planning, modification, tool execution, verification, and maintenance.
- **Financial terms:** Not disclosed.
- **Status:** Subject to customary closing conditions including regulatory approval.
- **OpenAI Codex metrics at announcement:** 2M+ weekly active users; 3× user growth since start of 2026; >70% month-over-month usage growth.
- **Open source:** OpenAI committed to continuing support for Astral's open-source products post-close.

*Sources: [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/19/openai-to-acquire-developer-tooling-startup-astral.html), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-astral/), [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/openai-astral-acquisition/)*

## Talent Moves

### OpenAI leadership departures

OpenAI is experiencing a significant leadership exodus coinciding with its transition from a nonprofit research structure to a for-profit entity:

- **Brad Lightcap** (longtime COO) — moved to "special projects" role
- **Fidji Simo** (key executive, architect of OpenAI's AGI product strategy) — on medical leave

Multiple reports describe talent migrating toward Anthropic and other competitors. The departures are framed as a tension between OpenAI's original research-first culture and new commercial imperatives ahead of a potential IPO.

*Source: [UnboxFuture](https://www.unboxfuture.com/2026/04/openai-executive-exodus-2026-leadership.html)*

### Keith Rabois joins Factory board

**Keith Rabois**, Managing Director at Khosla Ventures, joined Factory's board of directors as part of the Series C. Rabois has board or advisory roles across multiple enterprise AI companies.
