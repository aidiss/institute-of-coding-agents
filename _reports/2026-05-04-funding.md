## Acquisitions & Exits

### OpenAI acquires Astral *(Confirmed, Mar 19 2026)*
OpenAI agreed to acquire Astral, the startup behind widely used Python developer tooling — **uv** (package/project manager), **Ruff** (linter/formatter), and **ty** (type checker). The tools collectively reached hundreds of millions of downloads per month. Astral founder **Charlie Marsh** and the full team join OpenAI's Codex team. Financial terms were not disclosed. Regulatory approval still pending at time of announcement. Strategic rationale: deepen Codex across the full software lifecycle — planning, editing, tooling, and verification.

### SpaceX acquires option to buy Cursor for $60B *(Confirmed, Apr 21 2026)*
SpaceX (which merged with xAI in Feb 2026) struck a deal giving it the right to acquire Cursor (Anysphere) for **$60 billion** by end of 2026, or alternatively pay **$10 billion** for joint AI coding and knowledge-work development. If SpaceX opts not to acquire, it owes a $10B breakup fee. The deal was announced by both companies and reported by CNBC and Bloomberg; it preempted the in-progress $2B standalone fundraise. Context: Cursor's valuation has compressed the timeline from $2.5B (Jan 2025) → $9.9B (Jun 2025) → $29.3B (Nov 2025) → $50B+ (Apr 2026 talks) in roughly 15 months.

### Google licenses Windsurf tech, hires leadership *(Completed, mid-2025)*
After OpenAI's $3B acquisition of Windsurf (formerly Codeium) collapsed, Google paid **$2.4B** in a licensing deal: $1.2B to investors and $1.2B in compensation packages for ~40 employees, including co-founders **Varun Mohan** and **Douglas Chen**, who joined Google.

### Cognition acquires Windsurf IP & remaining staff *(Confirmed, Dec 2025)*
Following the Google deal, Cognition AI acquired Windsurf's remaining product and IP for approximately **$250M**, retaining all staff not hired by Google.

## Context & Trends

- **Coding is the highest-funded AI vertical**: Developer tooling startups (Cognition, Poolside, Replit, Magic, Augment, Codeium, Factory, StackBlitz) collectively raised over **$3B** across the 2025–2026 cycle.
- **Average round size doubling**: Among agentic AI startups closing rounds in Q4 2025 / early 2026, the average was $155M — nearly double the $82M average for H1 2025.
- **Enterprise GTM is the new front**: OpenAI and Anthropic are aggressively hiring enterprise sales talent from incumbent SaaS companies; the enterprise segment is viewed as the most profitable and defensible growth area.
- **Consolidation accelerating**: The Windsurf saga (OpenAI bid → Google acqui-hire → Cognition IP acquisition) illustrates a maturing market where distribution and talent matter as much as models.
