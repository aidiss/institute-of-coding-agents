### Revenue & Valuation Context

- **Cursor / Anysphere**: fastest B2B company from zero to $2B ARR (~3 years). ARR trajectory: $100M (Jan 2025) → $500M (Jun 2025) → $1B (Nov 2025) → $2B (Feb 2026). Forecasting $6B ARR by end of 2026. Previous valuation: ~$60B (Mar 2026 round on $2B ARR); current talks at $50B *pre-money* for a new $2B raise.
- **Cognition AI**: ARR grew from $1M (Sep 2024) to $73M (Jun 2025). Last confirmed valuation: $10.2B (Sep 2025 round, $400M led by Founders Fund). Proposed new valuation of $25B would be a 2.5× step-up. Growth attributed in large part to the Windsurf product acquisition (see below).
- **Factory AI**: differentiates on model-agnostic routing (Anthropic Claude, Chinese models, etc.) for enterprise engineering teams.
- **Emergent**: $50M ARR, 5M+ users across 190+ countries; targets vibe-coding / low-code segment.
- **Replit**: 10-year journey from browser IDE to vibe-coding platform; $9B valuation represents significant step-up.

## Acquisitions & Exits

### Windsurf (Codeium) — Three-way split (2025–2026)

The most consequential deal in the space spanned three parties:

1. **OpenAI** agreed to acquire Windsurf for ~$3B (Apr 2025). Deal collapsed when Microsoft blocked the transfer of full IP ownership due to exclusivity clauses in its OpenAI partnership.
2. **Google** stepped in with a $2.4B licensing + talent deal (Jul 2025). Google received a non-exclusive technology license and hired co-founders **Varun Mohan** and **Douglas Chen** plus ~40 senior employees into Google DeepMind. The $2.4B split roughly evenly: ~$1.2B to Windsurf investors, ~$1.2B in compensation packages for the ~40 hires (a substantial share to Mohan and Chen).
3. **Cognition** acquired the remainder — Windsurf's product, brand, IP, and remaining team — shortly after the Google announcement. Cognition waived vesting cliffs for all Windsurf employees who joined. Since the acquisition, Cognition's ARR more than doubled, underpinning the current $25B fundraise talks.

### Meta acqui-hires Dreamer AI (Mar 23, 2026)

Meta acquired the co-founders and team of **Dreamer**, an agentic AI startup, for an undisclosed sum. The team joined **Meta Superintelligence Labs**. Notable: former Meta/Google executive **Hugo Barra** is among those who joined Meta through this deal.

### OpenAI — 6 acquisitions in Q1 2026

OpenAI closed six acquisitions in early 2026, nearly matching its full 2025 total. Confirmed deals include an acqui-hire of **OpenClaw**, an open-source AI agent built by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger. OpenAI's acquisition pace is under growing FTC scrutiny (see below).

## Regulatory Notes

- **FTC** (Chair Andrew Ferguson) announced in Jan 2026 an investigation into acqui-hires across the tech industry, citing concern they are "mergers in disguise" that evade antitrust review. The Microsoft–Inflection AI deal is already under active FTC investigation.
- Congressional scrutiny of acqui-hire structures is ongoing.
