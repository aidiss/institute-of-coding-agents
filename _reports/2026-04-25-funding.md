### Notes

- **Anysphere (Cursor)** hit $2B ARR in February 2026 — the fastest B2B software company ever to reach that milestone — and is projecting $6B+ ARR by year-end. The proposed $50B pre-money valuation is nearly a 2× step-up from the November 2025 round, which itself came just five months after a $900M raise in June 2025. No lead investor has been named publicly; terms remain fluid.

- **Cognition AI** would more than double its September 2025 valuation of $10.2B. Devin's ARR grew from $1M (Sep 2024) to $73M (Jun 2025). The company absorbed Windsurf's product, IP, and ~210 engineers in December 2025 for ~$250M — adding significant product surface area ahead of this round.

- **Factory AI** targets enterprise engineering teams with AI "droids" that handle complete ticket-to-deployment workflows. The Khosla-led round validates enterprise appetite for autonomous software agents beyond IDE copilots.

- **Replit** tripled its valuation in six months (Sep 2025 → Mar 2026). The company targets $1B ARR by end of 2026. Angel backers include Shaquille O'Neal and Jared Leto, adding consumer-culture credibility to the "vibe coding" narrative.

## Acquisitions & Exits

### Meta / Dreamer — Acqui-hire (Mar 23, 2026, Confirmed)

Meta signed the co-founders and personnel of Dreamer, an agentic AI startup, for an undisclosed sum. The team — including Hugo Barra (former VP at Meta and Google) — joined Meta's **Superintelligence Labs** division. Deal continues the pattern of acqui-hire structures used to sidestep antitrust review.

### OpenAI / OpenClaw — Acqui-hire (Feb 2026, Confirmed)

OpenAI hired Peter Steinberger, the Austrian developer behind the open-source **OpenClaw** agent, to work on its Codex team. No IP acquisition; Steinberger was reportedly courted by every major lab. OpenAI has now completed six acquisitions in the first quarter of 2026, nearly matching its full-year 2025 total.

### Nvidia / Groq Talent Deal (2026, Confirmed)

Nvidia hired Groq founder and CEO **Jonathan Ross**, president **Sunny Madra**, and the majority of Groq's engineering team. The deal was structured as a licensing agreement but is widely characterised as an acqui-hire. Groq retains its corporate entity; the FTC has flagged the structure.

### Context: Windsurf — Three-Way Split (Dec 2025, previously reported)

For completeness: Cognition acquired Windsurf's product and IP for ~$250M in December 2025, after an earlier Google DeepMind talent deal (Windsurf CEO Varun Mohan + ~40 engineers, ~$2.4B total package) and a collapsed OpenAI $3B acquisition blocked by Microsoft.

## Context

Q1 2026 saw a record $300B in global venture investment across ~6,000 startups; AI accounted for $242B (~80%). The AI coding agent sub-sector is the single largest category by enterprise spend growth (+320% YoY). Acqui-hire structures continue to dominate M&A, allowing acquirers to absorb elite teams while avoiding the merger-review timelines that full acquisitions trigger. The FTC and congressional panels are actively scrutinising the pattern; no enforcement action has been announced as of this report date.
