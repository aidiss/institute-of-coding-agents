## Acquisitions & Exits

### Windsurf (Codeium) — Three-way split (Jul 2025)
A complex deal that resolved over a single weekend:

1. **OpenAI** had agreed to acquire Windsurf for $3B; Microsoft reportedly blocked the deal over concerns about exclusivity clauses in its existing partnership.
2. **Google** paid Windsurf $2.4B in a licensing deal — $1.2B to investors, $1.2B in compensation packages for ~40 Windsurf employees including CEO Varun Mohan.
3. **Cognition AI** then acquired Windsurf's remaining IP, product, trademark, and brand for an estimated $250M. Deal was negotiated from Friday evening to Monday morning.

As of Mar 2026, Windsurf (now under Cognition) moved to quota-based pricing; claims 59% of Fortune 500 companies using it in some capacity.

### Meta acqui-hires Dreamer (Mar 23, 2026)
Meta signed up the co-founders and team of Dreamer, an agentic AI startup. Hugo Barra (former Meta VP and Google exec) and the Dreamer team join Meta's Superintelligence Labs. Price undisclosed.

### OpenAI acquisition spree (Jan–Feb 2026)
OpenAI closed six acquisitions in early 2026, approaching its full-2025 total. Notable:
- **OpenClaw** (Feb 2026): Acqui-hire of open-source AI agent built by Austrian developer Peter Steinberger.
- **Convogo**, **Torch Health**, **Crixet** — announced Jan 2026; primarily consulting/tools plays rather than pure coding agents.
